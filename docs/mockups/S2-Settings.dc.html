Your protection, in one picture

Fixed for this prototype. You borrow at 50% of your collateral's value. If the price falls and the ratio climbs past 70%, the agent sells a little collateral to bring it back to 50%, never more than 20% of your collateral per day.

Reduce above 70%

Liquidation 86%
Morpho's rule, not ours. Protection is not a guarantee.

The agent will

  • Reduce your debt when the ratio passes 70%.
  • Move the position to the approved backup Morpho market if the live one becomes unsafe.
  • Exit, repaying everything from collateral, if nothing else works.

At most one action per 120 seconds.

The agent can never

  • Increase your debt on this venue.
  • Sell more than 20% of your collateral in a day.
  • Send funds anywhere except repaying your loan.
  • Change these limits.

Enforced by the vault's key restrictions and the Executor contract, not by the agent's good will.

You keep

  • Every other action: only your signature moves funds.
  • The right to close at any time by repaying.
  • A 10-minute expiry on every message you sign.

Which agent build you approve is checked for you on the next screens, against the values published on GitHub.
